**Pluto Retrograde — What's In Your Hidden Tab**
- Source: Cultural Darkness (🔴 GAP FILL — first Cultural Darkness email of cycle) + bridges Witch Victory canon
- Angle: Wednesday May 6 Pluto stations retrograde in Aquarius until Oct 15. Astrology line everyone is posting: "what rises from the shadows." Julie has been quietly learning astrology — she pulls out the chart at her kitchen table on Saturday morning, reads what Pluto retrograde means, and translates it into Skullette terms. Pluto retrograde isn't shadow-work in the abstract — it's the bag in your hidden tab. The cart you closed nine times. The wishlist you've never spoken aloud. Yesterday Jennifer in the Bible Belt typed it out: "ALL OF MY FAVORITE DESIGNS. ALL OF THEM." That's Pluto retrograde. The private list refuses to stay private.
- Hook: "Wednesday Pluto goes retrograde. I'm a beginner at this — Alice keeps sending me PDFs — but I read enough this morning to know what it means for our witches." (or open with Julie at the kitchen table with the chart printed)
- Turn: Astrology says "shadow work, hidden truths, what rises from the depths." Julie's translation: it's the tab you've kept open since February. Every Skullette reader has one. The reader is not the abstract — she IS the chart.
- Pivot: MD sale up to 70% off + ship-cutoff math (Standard May 5 / Express May 7). "Pluto already opened the tab. The ship cutoff closes it Tuesday."
- Product: Full MD collection. Soft-feature whichever bag has been hot — Lunacat / Nyxrose / Mournaire backpack — as "the bag everyone's tab has been on."
- Pillar: 1 (Polarization / Brand Philosophy)
- Law: #9 Pop Culture Tie-In + #15 Intimate Details + #1 Personality
- New canon to establish: Julie học chiêm tinh — beginner-tier, learning from Alice (or some other ensemble character — TBD in /write-email). Reusable beat for future moon-phase / retrograde / sabbat content. Anchors a fresh Julie-Personal sub-canon (the witch who was supposed to know astrology already, but is actually a beginner — humanizing reversal).
- Callbacks: Jennifer Bible Belt "ALL OF THEM" (May 1 canon) used as Pluto-retrograde archetype proof. Optional: Beltane skip from May 1 (didn't run that email but the Scorpio Full Moon was that night) becomes the "I missed it but I'm catching up" beat.
- Notes: Cultural-Darkness-FIRST email — DOES NOT mirror Witch Victory entry frames. Julie is IN the scene at her kitchen table reading. Customer (Jennifer) is REFERENCED, not featured. Window: works May 2-5. Pluto stations May 6 — after that, energy shifts to "we're already in it." If user delays to May 3-4, reframe slightly toward "Pluto stations in 3 days / 2 days."
- Caution: Don't overdo the astrology-jargon. Julie is a BEGINNER — readers who don't follow astrology can't be alienated. Translate every term. "Stations retrograde" = "starts going backwards." "Aquarius" = "the sign we're under right now." Keep it 2 AM witchy-best-friend voice, not yoga-teacher voice. If we use the word "shadow work" once, we earn it; twice, we're Brenda's yoga teacher.
