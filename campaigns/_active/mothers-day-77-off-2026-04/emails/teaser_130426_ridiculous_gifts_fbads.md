Because I realized I had one too.

I was eight. I made my mom a "protection potion." Mason jar, tap water, red food coloring, a handful of glitter from the craft drawer, and one dead ladybug I found on the windowsill. She kept it. Twenty-something years. A jar of food dye and a dead bug.
