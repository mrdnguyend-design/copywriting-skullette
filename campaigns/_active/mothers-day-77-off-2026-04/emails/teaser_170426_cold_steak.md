**Subject Line:** A cold steak decided for me
**Preview Text:** Grey all the way through. Now Alice has a number.

---

I had the worst steak of my life last night.

At my favorite restaurant. The one I tell everyone is "the closest thing to magic." Packed wall to wall. Couldn't hear the person across from me.

Fine. Cool. Busy night.

Food came out. I cut into the steak.

No steam. No pink. Just grey.

All the way through.

The kind of grey that happens when a kitchen is pushing out forty plates it can really only do twenty of.

I looked up.

Tickets stacking in the window. Cooks not moving fast, just moving. One of them sweating into his own forearm. The owner at the bar. Grinning. Greeting two MORE tables.

He was watching his kitchen drown and grinning about reservations.

And my plate, my magic restaurant plate, had stopped mattering twenty plates ago.

(I wasn't mad at the cooks. I was mad at the man who knew his limit and chose the money instead.)

---

Drove home.

Couldn't shake it.

By the time I pulled into my driveway, it wasn't about steak anymore.

It was about my sale.

Because in a few days the sale goes live. You've been hearing about it. **77% off. Wallets from $9.95. Bags from $29.95.** Skull hardware. Wine-dark linings. Nyxrose. Lunacat. A couple more we haven't shown yet.

**April 22, 23, 24.** Three days. Private. Registered witches only.

And somewhere inside those three days is a version of this sale where we say yes to too many tables, my team starts rushing, and somebody's mom opens a box with a loose skull clasp because we were moving too fast to catch it.

I can see the box.

I can see the mom.

I can see the "*it's fine, honey*" text she sends her daughter after.

That text stays with a daughter for a very long time.

---

This morning I called Alice in before the coffee was even cold.

(Mine wasn't finished either. That's how you know it's serious.)

"Alice. How many can we ship without rushing a clasp? Without one lining sitting crooked?"

Alice did not look up from her laptop.

"A thousand."

Not *roughly* a thousand. Not *around* a thousand.

A thousand.

Then she pulled up her second laptop. Carrier page. She had already called them.

Of course she had.

Mother's Day week, every carrier in the country is slammed. A thousand packages they'll guarantee land by **May 10th.** Past that?

*"We'll try."*

I know what "we'll try" means.

"We'll try" means some witch's mom finds the box on May 11th. One day late. Smiles. Texts her daughter, *"it's fine, honey."*

And I am not being the person who "we'll tried" somebody's mother. Not on Mother's Day.

So we cap at 1,000. Because that's what Alice can ship without rushing a clasp, and what the carrier can land by May 10. Anything past 1,000 would be the restaurant.
