### W15 (Apr 6 – Apr 12) — FB Ads Launch + First Warmup

- **Email:** Daily infotainment × 5, then Teaser #1 (Apr 12)
- **SMS:** HIGH daily + REG warmup starts Apr 10 (500 sends)
- **FB Ads:** Start Apr 10 (~$462/day)
- **MASS SMS:** Apr 12 (40,400 sends) — first MASS touch
- **Content focus:** Stories through Apr 9, transition to MD teasers
- **Notes:** Apr 10 is pivot day — FB Ads + first REG warmup

### W16 (Apr 13 – Apr 19) — Teaser Blitz

- **Email:** Teaser #2-#7 daily (53,494 sends each)
- **SMS:** HIGH daily + REG warmup ramp (1,200→2,200) + MASS every other day (~41K sends)
- **FB Ads:** Running (~$462/day)
- **Content focus:** Mother's Day angles — 7 consecutive teasers
- **Notes:** Heaviest teaser week. REG warmup alternates HIGH+WU and MASS+WU days.

### W17 (Apr 19 – Apr 25) — SALE WEEK

- **Apr 19-21:** Final teasers + last call register (2,400 REG/day)
- **Apr 22 (Day 1):** SALE — Email MASS 48K + REG 4,500. SMS: 5×REG(22,500) + HIGH(7,970) + MASS(37K) + CF(1,000)
- **Apr 23 (Day 2):** SALE — Email MASS 48K + REG 5,500. SMS: 5×REG(27,500) + HIGH(7,970) + MASS(37K) + CF(500)
- **Apr 24 (Day 3):** SALE — Email MASS 48K + REG 6,000. SMS: 4×REG(24,000) + HIGH(7,970) + MASS(37K) + CF(300)
- **Apr 25:** Thank You email. Back to daily.
- **FB Ads:** End Apr 22
- **Notes:** Peak volume. REG list grows Day 1→3 (4,500→6,000). CF sends decrease each day.

### W18 (Apr 26 – May 2) — Post-Sale Cool Down

- **Email:** Daily infotainment × 7, April Recap (Apr 30)
- **SMS:** HIGH daily (now 10,370 = 7,970 + absorbed REG)
- **MASS SMS:** Apr 28 only (42K sends) — weekly touch
- **Content focus:** Stories, reflection, April recap
- **Notes:** HIGH list expanded. Return to regular rhythm.

1. **REG list build** — Need 5,500 by Apr 22. FB Ads ($6K) starts Apr 10 = 12 days to build. Monitor daily.
2. **SMS warmup deliverability** — 12-day ramp from 500→2,400. If engagement drops, reduce pace.
3. **MASS SMS list decay** — Losing 50-100/day on send days. Post-sale MASS ~37K (down from 39.6K).
4. **Sale SMS volume** — Day 1 alone = ~68,470 SMS sends. Postscript capacity OK?
5. **Post-sale engagement** — HIGH jumps 7,970→10,370. New HIGH members may need re-engagement.
